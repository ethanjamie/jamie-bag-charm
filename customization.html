Customization Process

From idea to finished product — our streamlined 4-step process makes custom leather bag charms simple.

1

Choose Your Style

Whether you prefer classic shapes or unique, eye-catching designs, we create custom leather bag charms in almost any form. Popular options include round, square, heart, star, and animal shapes, as well as leather flower bag charms. You can also send us your own shape or logo outline for a completely custom charm mold.

Round Square Heart Star Animal Flower Custom Shape
2

Pick Your Leather

Select from a wide range of premium leathers and finishes. Options include cross grain, litchi grain, flat grain, crocodile grain, snakeskin grain, and more. We also offer eco-friendly PU leather for vegan options. Each material is carefully sourced for durability, color vibrancy, and a high-quality feel.

Cross Grain Litchi Grain Flat Grain Crocodile Snakeskin Vegan PU
3

Add Your Branding

Personalize your bag charms with various processing options including embossing, debossing, foil stamping, UV printing, and laser engraving. These techniques allow your brand logo or message to stand out with premium detail and lasting quality.

Embossing Debossing Foil Stamping UV Printing Laser Engraving
4

Select Hardware

Complete your design with the perfect metal fittings. We offer split rings, lobster clasps, snap hooks, and custom-shaped hardware in finishes like gold, silver, black nickel, and antique brass — perfect for branded leather accessories.

Split Ring Lobster Clasp Snap Hook Gold Silver Black Nickel Antique Brass
Branding Methods

Make Your Mark

Choose from five professional branding methods to showcase your logo and create a lasting impression.

⬆️

Embossing

Creates a raised design on the leather surface. Adds a tactile, 3D effect that feels premium and looks sophisticated.

⬇️

Debossing

Presses the design into the leather for a recessed look. Subtle and elegant, perfect for minimalist brand identities.

✨

Foil Stamping

Metallic foil (gold, silver, rose gold) pressed into the leather. Creates a luxurious, eye-catching finish for logos.

🖨️

UV Printing

Full-color printing with UV-cured ink for vivid, detailed designs. Great for complex logos and multi-color artwork.

⚡

Laser Engraving

Permanent, precise engraving with a laser. Creates a clean, modern look with excellent detail and durability.

🎁

Custom Packaging

Complete your branded experience with custom packaging — kraft boxes, gift boxes, or printed packaging with your logo.
