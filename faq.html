Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our leather bag charms, customization, and ordering process.

Our MOQ starts at 100 pieces per design for most leather bag charms. For eco-friendly and specialized materials, the MOQ may be 200 pieces. We designed our low MOQ specifically to help boutique brands and startups get started without large upfront investment.
Absolutely! We specialize in branded leather bag charms. Your logo can be applied through embossing, debossing, foil stamping, UV printing, or laser engraving. We can create custom molds in your logo shape, or apply your logo to standard charm shapes.
Yes! We offer high-quality eco-friendly PU leather (vegan leather) and recycled leather options. These materials are durable, affordable, and perfect for brands committed to sustainability. Vegan leather options are available in all the same grain patterns and colors as our traditional leathers.
We can create almost any shape you can imagine! Popular options include geometric shapes (round, square, hexagon), hearts, stars, flowers, animals, letters, and custom logo outlines. Send us a sketch, reference image, or just describe your idea — our design team can bring it to life.
Standard production time is 15–30 days depending on order quantity, design complexity, and customization requirements. Rush orders may be available for an additional fee. We'll provide an accurate timeline with your quote.
Yes, we ship worldwide via FedEx, DHL, UPS, and other trusted couriers. Shipping costs and transit times vary by destination. We provide full tracking for every shipment and can arrange door-to-door delivery to most countries.
Yes! We strongly recommend samples before bulk production. Sample costs vary by design and can be credited towards your bulk order if you proceed. This lets you verify materials, colors, and branding before committing to larger quantities.
Pricing depends on several factors: charm size and shape, leather type (genuine vs. vegan), customization method (embossing, foil, UV, etc.), hardware selection, order quantity, and packaging requirements. Contact us with your specifications for a personalized quote within 24 hours.
Yes! We offer a variety of packaging solutions including kraft boxes, gift boxes, organza bags, and custom-printed packaging with your logo. Packaging can be plain (bulk) or branded for retail-ready presentation. Let us know your needs and we'll recommend the best options.
Absolutely. We use premium leather and high-quality hardware to ensure long-lasting quality, even with regular use. All charms undergo quality control testing for durability. Genuine leather develops a beautiful patina over time, while our vegan leather options are designed to maintain their appearance with minimal care.
We accept bank transfer (T/T), PayPal, Western Union, and Alibaba Trade Assurance for international orders. Payment terms typically require a deposit (30-50%) before production, with the balance due before shipping. Exact terms are specified in your quote.
Simply contact us through our contact form or email with your requirements: charm style, quantity, materials, and branding needs. Attach any reference images, sketches, or logo files. We'll respond within 24 hours with a quote and next steps. From there, we'll guide you through sample approval, production, and delivery.
